KARNATAKA STATE POLICE (KSP) - CYBER CRIME STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP) 2026:
1. Incident Response for Financial Phishing & UPI Fraud:
- Immediate freeze on suspect beneficiary bank accounts within the 2-hour Golden Window.
- Extraction of target UPI IDs, transaction hashes, IP logs, and ISP ASN routing tables.
- Electronic evidence must be hashed with SHA-256 immediately upon acquisition.

2. Indian Evidence Act Section 65B Certificate Mandate:
- All digital evidence (screenshots, web portal dumps, network trace logs) requires a Section 65B certificate for court admissibility.
- The certificate must specify device specifications, timestamp logs, and system integrity audits.

3. Zero FIR & Anonymous Reporting Protocol:
- Victims are entitled to file a Zero FIR at any Karnataka Police station without jurisdictional restriction.
- Citizens reporting cyber extortion, harassment, or financial scams may opt for anonymous filing with state level SCRB audit tracking.
